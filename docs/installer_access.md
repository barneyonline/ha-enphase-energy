# Requesting Enphase installer access

Some Enphase cloud features used by this integration require installer-level
permissions. Examples include Activation-based Grid Profile Control and some
installer diagnostics. A homeowner account may continue to work for the rest of
the integration while these features remain unavailable.

Enphase controls account roles and decides whether to grant installer access.
Completing training does not guarantee approval, and the required courses,
certifications, or business authorisations can vary by country and product. Only
request access for a system you own or are legally authorised and qualified to
service. Installer access does not replace electrical licensing, local regulatory
requirements, or product-specific commissioning certification.

## Suggested request process

1. Sign in to [Enphase University](https://university.enphase.com/my/) using the
   Enphase credentials associated with your existing Enlighten account. Depending
   on your region, Enphase may require an Enphase Installer Platform account or
   may need to link your existing account before the training catalogue is
   available.
2. Complete the current training and certification paths relevant to your system.
   These will typically include the **Enphase Energy System** and **IQ Energy
   Management** modules or their current regional equivalents. Complete any
   additional IQ Battery, System Controller, design, or commissioning courses
   shown as mandatory for your products and country.
3. Download the completion certificates from Enphase University.
4. Contact [Enphase Support](https://enphase.com/contact/support) using the online
   enquiry form, chat, or the phone number for your country. Ask for **Installer
   Toolkit (ITK) / Self Installer access** to be added to your existing Enlighten
   account. Include:

   - the email address registered to Enlighten and Enphase University;
   - the Enlighten Site ID for the system you are authorised to service;
   - the completed course names; and
   - the corresponding certificates as attachments.

5. Wait for Enphase Support to review the request and confirm the access grant.
   Support may request further training, proof of qualification, a company or
   installer-platform account, a design review, or other region-specific
   information.
6. After approval, sign out and back in to Enphase and reload this integration.
   Installer-only features are still shown only when the Enphase APIs confirm that
   the account has the required permission for that site.

## Suggested support request

> **Subject:** Request Installer Toolkit (ITK) / Self Installer access
>
> Please add Installer Toolkit (ITK) / Self Installer access to my existing
> Enlighten account. I have completed the applicable Enphase Energy System and IQ
> Energy Management training and have attached my certificates.
>
> Enlighten email: [your registered email]
>
> Site ID: [your site ID]
>
> Completed certifications: [course names]

Send account details and certificates only to Enphase through its official support
channels. Do not include them in public Home Assistant logs, diagnostics, issues,
or discussions.

## Official references

- [Enphase training and certification](https://enphase.com/installers/training-and-certifications)
- [Enphase University](https://university.enphase.com/my/)
- [Enphase Installer App training](https://enphase.com/installers/training/videos)
- [Enphase Support contact selector](https://enphase.com/contact/support)

Enphase describes its certifications as product-specific and notes that mandatory
certifications unlock the corresponding installer-platform features. Enphase also
identifies the Enphase Installer App as the former Installer Toolkit (ITK). The
support form accepts the registered email address, Site ID, request details, and
attachments. Enphase does not currently publish a single global policy guaranteeing
Self Installer access after particular courses, so Support's response is
authoritative.
